-------------------PS4 Pipeline Launch------------------

- Turn on husky and wait a few minutes
- Connect a terminal to Husky to initiate master node
- Turn on ps4 remote and place Husky in position
- Run the commands below


                   ---On Jetson---
export ROS_MASTER_URI=http://192.168.131.1:11311
export ROS_IP=192.168.131.137
roslaunch obstacle_avoidance ps4_pipeline.launch --screen
---> Options: run
---> Share: kill


ctrl-c, or red button if conenction dies or behavior is irradic
